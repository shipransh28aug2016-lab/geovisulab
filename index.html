Earth Interior & Discontinuities (Class XI Ch. 3)

Earth consists of concentric shells distinguished by density and seismic wave velocity changes:

1. Continental & Oceanic Crust 0 – 35 km

Mean density: 2.7 g/cm³ (Sial) to 3.0 g/cm³ (Sima). Bounded at base by the Mohorovičić Discontinuity (Moho).

2. Mantle & Asthenosphere 35 – 2,900 km

Upper mantle includes weak ductile Asthenosphere (100–200 km) upon which tectonic plates glide. Bounded at base by Gutenberg Discontinuity.

3. Outer & Inner Core 2,900 – 6,371 km

Liquid outer core (blocks S-waves) and solid inner core (Nife: Nickel + Iron, density up to 13 g/cm³). Bounded by Lehmann Discontinuity at 5,150 km.
