      Earth Interior & Discontinuities (Class XI Ch. 3)

      1. Crust (Sial / Sima) 0 – 35 km

      Mean density: 2.7 to 3.0 g/cm³. Bounded at base by the Mohorovičić Discontinuity (Moho).

      2. Mantle & Asthenosphere 35 – 2,900 km

      Upper mantle includes weak ductile Asthenosphere (100–200 km) upon which tectonic plates glide. Bounded at base by Gutenberg Discontinuity.

      3. Outer & Inner Core 2,900 – 6,371 km

      Liquid outer core (blocks S-waves completely creating 105°–145° shadow zone) and solid inner core (Nife, density up to 13 g/cm³). Bounded by Lehmann Discontinuity at 5,150 km.
