# Third-party data

The MIT licence covers this repository's code. Bundled data keeps its own licence:

- **Cliopatria** (Seshat Global History Databank, v0.2.1) — CC BY 4.0. Filtered to the Ganga–Magadha corridor, coordinates rounded; boundaries are approximate and unreviewed. https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria
- **Natural Earth** — Public Domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com
- Leaflet (BSD-2), Three.js (MIT), MapLibre GL (BSD-3): see their packages.
- **Earth textures** (`public/textures/earth_*.jpg`) — from Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), CC BY 4.0, resized/merged as distributed in the three.js examples.
